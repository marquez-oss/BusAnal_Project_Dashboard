Key Agricultural Insights

  • Fertilizer Volatility: Urea and Complete prices peaked dramatically in 2022 due to global supply shocks before stabilizing toward 2025–2026.
  • Input Cost Impact: Fertilizer costs account for roughly 15-20% of total palay cash production expenses per hectare.
  • Retail Price Trends: Well-milled rice hovered around ₱43-₱45/kg during 2019-2022 before rising to ₱49-₱55/kg range in recent harvests.
